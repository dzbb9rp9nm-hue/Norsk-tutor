Privacy

Norsk Tutor is a small, independently built app for practising spoken Norwegian. It has no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and your data is never sold. This page says plainly what the app handles and where it goes. Last updated 12 November 2026.

Your voice

Speech is turned into text by your device's own speech services — your browser's, or Apple's in the iPhone app — which may use their provider's servers to do so, under that provider's terms. Norsk Tutor itself never receives, stores or keeps a recording of your voice. Only the recognised text is used, and only to carry the conversation.

Your conversations

The text of your practice conversation is sent to Anthropic's Claude service to generate the tutor's replies — either through this app's own server, or directly if you use a personal API key. Conversations are used for nothing else: no advertising, no profiling.

Your account, if you create one

An account holds your email address, a password, and your saved learning (sessions, phrases, lesson progress) so it can follow you between devices. It is stored with Supabase, a managed database service. Without an account, everything stays in your browser's own storage on your device.

Your control

You can export your learning, delete saved sessions and phrases, and sign out at any time from inside the app. Browser-only learning can be removed by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
